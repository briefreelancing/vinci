Why this piece

This is the largest recipe topic in the list: 5 keywords with 68,500 monthly searches between them, led by "sourdough bread recipe" at 40,500. Average difficulty is 51.8, so it will take a strong page to compete. A page already exists for it, so this is a rewrite, not a new post.

What the searcher wants

The searcher wants to bake a loaf at home and needs a recipe they can follow start to finish. Several of the 5 keywords add "easy" or "for beginners", so many of them have not baked sourdough before and want reassurance as much as instructions.

Who it is for

Home bakers making their first or second sourdough loaf. They own basic kitchen equipment and may not have a starter yet.

Angle

Written by the bakers who make this loaf for the shop every morning. Lead with what a professional watches for at each stage, and what to do when it goes wrong, which generic recipes skip.
